# Campus Customs storefront design (Problem 10)

- **Product-led hero:** The home page pairs oversized editorial type and deep Yale-blue layers with a tilted, clickable Basic Hoodie product print. A shopper sees a real item immediately and can open its detail page from the first screen.
- **Clearer product presentation:** Catalogue cards now use consistent image framing, a small shop mark, separated price and “View piece” cues, and a restrained lift on hover. The details and next action are easier to scan across a large catalogue.
- **Distinctive but controlled motion:** Buttons, cards, the hero print, and the chat panel respond to interaction; reduced-motion settings disable the animation. Feedback makes the shop feel responsive without distracting from products.
- **More inviting chat:** The floating assistant uses a branded concierge header, readable message bubbles, formatted emphasis, a visible thinking state, and page-aware suggested questions. These cues make it easier to start a useful product or stock conversation.
- **Storefront copy:** About Us now describes the working assistant, and the footer uses customer-facing location and brand language. This removes development-era wording from the shopping experience.

The production build passed. The live desktop home page, product-led hero, chat panel, and 390 px mobile layout were visually checked. A mobile chat-width issue found during review was fixed.
